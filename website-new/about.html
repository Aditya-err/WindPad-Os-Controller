The Story

WindPad was born from a simple frustration: why should controlling your computer require installing yet another background service, dealing with network configurations, or relying on proprietary apps that track your data?

The answer seemed obvious — leverage the Bluetooth HID protocol that every operating system already supports. By making your Android phone appear as a standard mouse and keyboard, you eliminate the middleman entirely.

What started as a personal project to control a media center PC has evolved into a comprehensive input solution used by developers, content creators, students, and accessibility users worldwide.
