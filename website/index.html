Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything on my PC?

No! Windpad uses standard Bluetooth HID profiles. Your PC thinks it's a real, physical bluetooth mouse.

Does it work over Wi-Fi?

Windpad is specifically designed for Bluetooth for the lowest possible latency and maximum compatibility without network restrictions.

Is it completely free?

Yes, Windpad is 100% free and Open Source. No ads, no tracking.

What is the "Windows Helper" app for?

The Helper is completely optional. It just provides advanced pointer smoothing for Windows machines if you want an even better experience.
