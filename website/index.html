THE DIFFERENCE

Not another remote app.

WindPad uses Bluetooth HID, so compatible devices recognize your phone as a standard mouse and keyboard.

Traditional Remote Apps

Wi-Fi & Companion App Required

  • Requires background server app installed on PC
  • Both devices must be on the same local Wi-Fi
  • High latency, firewall blocks, network lag
  • Bloated with ads, subscriptions, and telemetry
The WindPad Standard

Hardware Bluetooth HID

  • Zero PC software — recognized as a physical mouse
  • No Wi-Fi needed — works 100% offline anywhere
  • Ultra-low latency direct radio frequency link
  • 100% Free & Open Source with zero tracking
