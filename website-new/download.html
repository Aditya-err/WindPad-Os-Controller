System Requirements

WindPad works with most modern Android devices and any Bluetooth-enabled computer.

Android Device

  • Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or higher
  • Bluetooth 4.0 or higher
  • 50MB free storage space
  • Bluetooth permissions enabled

Target Computer

  • Windows, macOS, or Linux
  • Bluetooth capability
  • No additional software required
  • Standard HID driver support

Permissions

  • Bluetooth & Bluetooth Admin
  • Location (for Bluetooth scanning only)
  • Foreground Service (keep connection alive)
  • No internet access required

Troubleshooting

Common issues and their solutions.

Ensure Bluetooth is enabled on both devices. Try restarting Bluetooth on both devices. If using Android 12+, make sure location permissions are granted for Bluetooth scanning.

Unpair the device from both your phone and PC. Restart Bluetooth on both devices. Try pairing from your PC's Bluetooth settings menu instead of from WindPad.

Check for interference from other Bluetooth devices. Ensure both devices have sufficient battery. Try moving closer to reduce distance. On Android, check that WindPad has background battery optimization disabled.

WindPad uses hardware Bluetooth for minimal latency. If you experience lag, try adjusting the sensitivity and DPI settings in the app or ensuring your device is within standard Bluetooth range.
